** This protocol integrates and adapt methods from [Mass et al., 2016](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.2016.0322) and [Huffmyer Larval settlement 2021 protocol](https://ahuffmyer.github.io/ASH_Putnam_Lab_Notebook/Larval-Settlement-Mcapitata-June-2021/), for the context of this pre-liminary experiment

** Goal: to collect replicate pools of 3 morphological larvae stages from 3 different Caribean coral species.

## Materials and Equipment
o	Conditioned plugs (ideally conditioned for 3 months)
o	Small Incubator capable of heating 22°C to 32°C
o	Lights on the incubator 
o	Stereoscope
o	Plastics: Small plastic jars (4 per species),  1 ml micropipettes, 1.5 ml microcentrifuge tubes, 6- well plates for counting 
o	RNA/DNA shield
o	Driller and drill bits.

## Getting things ready prior to Larvae arrival (3 months in advance)

1. Calculate the number of plugs needed, take in consideration # of plugs per replicate, # species. In this case 3 plugs per replicate as the plastic jars are small, 4 coral species, 3 replicates per species plus a control (without conditioned plugs). In this case 36 conditioned plugs + extra just in case.
2. 80 plugs were put to be conditioned in May, blue tanks in the wetlab. Seawater 34PSU, Temperature 24°C, coral species in the tank Pocillopora, Montipora and Porites.
